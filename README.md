# 🌍 AeroCare AI

### AI-Powered Environmental Health Intelligence Platform

AeroCare AI is an intelligent environmental health monitoring platform that analyzes real-time environmental conditions and provides personalized health and safety recommendations based on a user's health profile.

The platform combines **environmental telemetry, health risk assessment, and Generative AI** to help users understand how air quality, weather, humidity, and UV exposure may affect their daily activities.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Environmental conditions such as air pollution, high temperatures, humidity, and UV radiation can significantly affect human health.

AeroCare AI analyzes these environmental factors and combines them with a user's personal health profile to generate a personalized environmental health risk assessment.

The platform provides:

- 🌡️ Real-time weather analysis
- 🌫️ Air Quality Index (AQI) monitoring
- ☀️ UV exposure tracking
- 💧 Humidity analysis
- 🫁 Personalized health risk assessment
- 🤖 AI-generated health recommendations
- 🕒 Best time recommendations for outdoor activities

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

People are constantly exposed to changing environmental conditions such as:

- Poor air quality
- High PM2.5 levels
- Extreme humidity
- High UV radiation
- Temperature changes
- Weather variations

However, most weather and air-quality applications provide general environmental information without considering an individual's health condition.

For example, the same AQI level may affect:

- A healthy adult
- A child
- A senior citizen
- A person with asthma
- A person with a heart condition

very differently.

### AeroCare AI solves this problem by providing personalized environmental health intelligence.

---

# 💡 Proposed Solution

AeroCare AI combines environmental data with user health information to calculate a personalized health risk score.

The system analyzes:

### User Profile

- Age Group
- Health Condition
- Lifestyle
- Outdoor Exposure

### Environmental Conditions

- Temperature
- Humidity
- Air Quality Index
- PM2.5
- PM10
- Ozone (O₃)
- Nitrogen Dioxide (NO₂)
- Carbon Monoxide (CO)
- UV Index
- Weather Conditions

Based on this information, AeroCare AI generates:

- Personalized Risk Score
- Risk Level
- Primary Environmental Risk Driver
- Health Precautions
- Biological Impact Analysis
- Outdoor Activity Recommendations
- AI-Powered Personalized Health Advice
